# Credits and third-party material

## Spoken by rustykey

The original Spoken project and voice packs are the work of **rustykey (Rusty Key)** and the Spoken contributors. DMSpeaks Lite builds on that work by preparing and selecting personalized dialogue for playback in WoW. The kit includes dialogue fragments derived from Spoken recordings. [Spoken project](https://github.com/rusty-key/spoken-wow) · [Spoken voice packs](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken)

Spoken licenses its original project code under MIT. Its third-party notice identifies Blizzard's quest dialogue and game artwork, credits the artists who created project assets, and describes the terms associated with the generated sound packs. Each rights holder’s terms apply to its material. The DMSpeaks MIT license covers DMSpeaks contributors’ original code and documentation. [Spoken license](https://github.com/rusty-key/spoken-wow/blob/master/LICENSE) · [Spoken’s third-party notice](https://github.com/rusty-key/spoken-wow/blob/master/THIRD_PARTY.md)

## World of Warcraft and speech providers

World of Warcraft is a trademark of Blizzard Entertainment. Blizzard retains its rights in its quest text and game artwork. The speech service selected by a player provides the generated take for that player’s character. Reuse of a recording depends on its source material and the terms applicable to that generation.

## Windows components

The installer includes license notices for its Python runtime and libraries, FFmpeg, PocketSphinx, eSpeak NG, NSIS, and other bundled components. The runtime list identifies the installed notice locations. [Windows runtime notices](THIRD-PARTY-RUNTIMES.md)
